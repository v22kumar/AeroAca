# Advanced modules: crosswalk between the original 2.0 proposal and the four-pathway structure

Date: 2026-11-06. Reconciles two documents that were written a few hours apart without seeing each other: the **original advanced syllabus** (`ADVANCED_SYLLABUS_2.0_PROPOSAL.md`, modules 17 to 25, approved in principle) and the **engineering audit** (`ENGINEERING_GAP_AUDIT.md`, `LEARNING_COVERAGE_MATRIX.md`, `ENGINEERING_COVERAGE.csv`, `CAREER_PATHWAYS.md`), whose "provisional groupings 17 to 25" used the same numbers for partly different subjects.

## Decision rules

1. **The original identifiers 17 to 25 are preserved** with their original names and scope. Nothing is renumbered or silently replaced. Public slugs and progress ids are unaffected (none of these modules exist in `content/curriculum.ts` yet).
2. **Subjects the original proposal did not cover** (control engineering beyond orientation, estimation and sensor fusion, engineering mathematics and ML foundations, edge AI, autonomy and simulation, technical product ownership) become **new modules 26 to 31**, named below. They are additions, not replacements.
3. **Where a provisional grouping overlaps an original module**, its subjects are assigned to the original module and the provisional label is retired. The CSV and matrix now carry the reconciled module numbers; the word "provisional" no longer appears in a module cell.
4. **Shared subjects are mapped once.** A subject that belongs to a foundation module and to an advanced module carries both numbers (for example `8;20`): the foundation module holds the entry lessons, the advanced module the depth. There is one lesson, not two.
5. **Sequence is governed by the implementation plan**, not by module number. Module 17 is the lowest number and is P1-4 in the plan; module 23's first three lessons are P0-1b. Numbers name scope, batches name order.
6. **Principal direction.** Embedded and electronics R&D and aerospace systems engineering are the principal long-term direction. Module 17 (verification) is an important specialisation and a practical near-term door; it is one module of fifteen advanced ones, not the destination.
