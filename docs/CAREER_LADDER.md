# Career ladder: roles, reported pay, and the evidence that moves you up

Source of truth: `content/career-ladder.ts`, shown in the app on the Journey page ("Where this leads") and referenced from Today. This page is the same data in prose. Figures are in lakhs per year (LPA), India, as reported by public salary sites in November 2026. They are self-reported, the samples are small, titles differ between employers, and some figures are base pay while others are total pay. **They describe the market. Nothing here is a promise of what this academy, or any course, delivers.** The tests fail if the wording in the app ever does.

## The rungs

| Rung | Years | Roles | Reported band (LPA) | What the market asks for first |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Level 1: Engineer** (where the journey starts) | 2 to 4 | Avionics or systems integration engineer; V&V or test engineer; embedded software engineer; hardware validation engineer | **7 to 15** | Says exactly what she did at the right ownership level; explains the standards vocabulary from first principles; has one debugging story with a measurement that decided it |
| **Level 2: Senior or lead engineer** (what the track builds evidence for) | 4 to 8 | Senior systems or integration engineer; lead V&V engineer; senior embedded engineer; test automation lead; HIL or bench lead | **14 to 31** | Owns a component or bench end to end; answers "is it schedulable?", "what does the voter do?", "what did you inject?" with numbers; states every metric with its method; reviews others' work with independence |
| **Level 3: Principal engineer or architect** | 8 to 15 | Principal systems or software engineer; avionics architect; programme V&V lead; technical lead of a flight-control or mission-system team | **25 to 37** | Decides and defends architecture against the safety assessment; owns a programme's verification strategy; the person others bring the unexplained fault to; sets the standard for evidence |
| **Level 4: Chief engineer, technical fellow or engineering manager** | 15 and more | Chief engineer of a product line; technical fellow; engineering manager or head of V&V | **47 to 56** (thinnest data) | Accountable for a product's technical integrity across its life; judged on decisions made years earlier; known outside the company |

The target stated for this learner, ₹12 LPA and above, sits at the top of the Level 1 band and the bottom of Level 2. That is why the journey's stages 3 to 6 build Level 2 evidence: the market pays the Level 2 band for what an engineer can *show*, not for years counted.

## What was left out, and why

- Figures that looked like errors or outliers: a Glassdoor principal-engineer page showing a dollar figure in the crores; training-vendor pages quoting 45 to 85 LPA for "principal" roles with no sample; semiconductor verification figures, which are a different market from avionics V&V.
- Public-sector pay scales (for example at HAL) are published as scales, not surveys, and were not folded into the bands.
- Nothing about any specific employer of the learner. Company names here are public salary pages for large aerospace employers in India, chosen because they publish enough reports to be worth quoting.
