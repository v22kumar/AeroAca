# Associate Avionics Engineer — Cross-Industry Engineering Curriculum

The career headline is **Associate Avionics Engineer**. Training must extend to electronics R&D careers across industries. A professional headline does not replace an employer's official job title.

## A. Electronics & Hardware Engineering

1. **Electronic engineering fundamentals (P0):** circuit laws; passive and active components; op-amps, filters; ADC/DAC; logic; power regulation, grounding and protection.
2. **Circuit design, schematics and PCB (P0):** schematic reading; datasheets; reference designs; component selection; layout principles; signal integrity; EMI/EMC; KiCad and LTspice.
3. **Microcontrollers, processors and ARM (P0):** MCU/MPU/SoC; Cortex-M/R/A; STM32 peripherals; interrupts; timers; DMA; memory; boot and watchdog.
4. **Communication interfaces (P0):** UART, RS-232/485, I2C, SPI, CAN/CAN FD, LIN, Ethernet, TCP/UDP and Modbus; bus tracing and failure diagnosis.

## B. Embedded Software & Firmware

5. **Embedded C (P0):** types; functions; pointers; structures; bit manipulation; volatile/const/static; stack/heap; registers; small firmware exercises.
6. **Embedded C++ (P1):** classes, constructors, references, polymorphism, RAII, modular interfaces and debugging.
7. **Firmware architecture and drivers (P1):** startup, bootloader, HAL, BSP, polling/interrupt/DMA, peripherals, JTAG/SWD, GDB and update/recovery.
8. **RTOS & real-time systems (P0):** tasks, priorities, deadlines, scheduling, mutex/semaphore/queue, priority inversion, deadlocks and FreeRTOS basics.
9. **Embedded Linux (P1):** shell, filesystem, processes, boot, device tree, kernel/user space, driver basics, Git, build tools, logs and GDB.

## C. Product R&D, Integration & Debugging

10. **Instrumentation and fault diagnosis (P0):** DMM, oscilloscope, logic analyzer, bench PSU, DAQ, power/reset/clock, signal integrity, RCA.
11. **System integration & product engineering (P0):** requirements, architecture, interfaces, board bring-up, prototype integration, design reviews, changes and release.
12. **Verification, reliability & qualification (P0):** test design, traceability, integration/system/regression testing, ATE/jigs, SIL/HIL, environmental tests, FMEA.
13. **Python and test automation (P1):** Python basics, serial communications, SCPI/PyVISA, data acquisition, reports, pytest and reproducible testing.

## D. Cross-Industry Specialization

14. **Safety and standards awareness (P1):** DO-178C/DO-254, ISO 26262, ASPICE, AUTOSAR, IEC 60601, IEC 62304, ISO 13485, IEC 61508. Introduce each with its scope; do not claim certification.
15. **Applications (P2):** avionics/UAV, automotive ECU/HIL/diagnostics, medical electronics, PLC/motor control, semiconductor/FPGA, cameras/MIPI/GStreamer/RTSP.

## E. Interviews & Career

16. **Engineering communication and interviews (P0):** truthfully explain real responsibilities, project reasoning, design tradeoffs, troubleshooting, foundational coding, notice period and pay negotiation.

## 30-day interview fast track

| Days | Focus | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | Circuit fundamentals, schematics, MCU and measurements | Explain a board, identify power and clock faults |
| 6–10 | Embedded C, UART, SPI, I2C and CAN | Explain code and decode simple protocol traces |
| 11–15 | Integration, verification, RCA and traceability | Produce test cases, failure analysis and debug plan |
| 16–20 | RTOS, boot, firmware, Linux/driver concepts | Explain execution, concurrency and failure modes |
| 21–25 | ATE, HIL, Python and reliability | Build a small instrument/test workflow |
| 26–30 | Cross-industry cases and mock interviews | Present a truthful engineering portfolio exercise |

Start job applications and practice interviews from day one, independent of lesson completion. Learning advanced firmware does not create years of professional experience.

## Lesson design standard

Every short lesson must contain: **mission/problem → visual explanation → essential theory → engineering diagnosis or implementation exercise → feedback → interview questions → review card**. Cite reliable references in the content source where appropriate. Do not fabricate test measurements, standards requirements, or market claims.

## Portfolio project

**STM32-based sensor monitoring and fault detection system:** sensor acquisition, interface protocol, watchdog, diagnostic output, firmware, schematic, tests, fault-injection scenarios and engineering evidence. Build progressively from simulated demonstrations to real hardware. Do not upload confidential employer artifacts.
